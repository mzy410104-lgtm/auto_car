With 1 unit, pulse measurements provide speed magnitude, while the sign comes from inferred direction based on motion-control state. Commanded direction is not measured direction. Subsequent vehicle-speed conversion must use the actual rotational relationship between the sensed gear and the wheels; the transmission ratio and effective counts per revolution still need verification.

A stop-before-reversing control sequence is recommended. Receiving a reverse command must not immediately change the measured speed to a negative value. Mark direction as undetermined during braking, neutral, starting, and reversal transitions, and retain the raw pulses; unknown direction must not be treated as reliable zero speed. The stop-observation interval, minimum resolvable speed, and TBLE-04S reversal behavior require calibration on the actual vehicle; no unverified thresholds or delays are specified. A single photointerrupter cannot distinguish coasting or external pushing that disagrees with the control state. This design alone therefore cannot establish independent reverse-motion detection or successful navigation acceptance. ## 4. Connection Relationships

The following shows only the relationships between modules. It is not a pinout that can be wired directly.

```text
Existing computer (destination selection, map display)
             │ Network
Anker A1259 → Raspberry Pi 4B
                    ├─ USB → supplied RPLIDAR A1M8 adapter → lidar
                    └─ USB → NUCLEO-F446RE
                                  ├─ SPI → BNO085
                                  ├─ I²C → TCA9548A → four VL53L1X
                                  ├─ two counter inputs ← optical encoder
                                  └─ two PWM outputs → SN74AHCT125N → Pololu #2806
                                                                         ↓
FS-GT5 → FS-BS6 → manual PWM and mode selection ────────────────────────→ #2806
                                                                         ├─ S-U300 steering servo
                                                                         └─ WP-1060 control input

Carson 7.2V NiMH → fuse → mechanical traction-power main switch → WP-1060 → supplied motor
                                                                  └─ BEC → servo, receiver, and #2806 VM supply
```

- The NUCLEO uses its onboard ST-LINK USB port for power, debugging, and virtual serial communication; no additional ST-LINK or USB-UART is purchased. Use [ST's official UM1724](https://www.st.com/resource/en/user_manual/um1724-stm32-nucleo64-boards-mb1136-stmicroelectronics.pdf) for the actual serial interface and jumpers rather than copying the old CoVAPSy pin table.
- Connect the receiver to the #2806 master inputs and the buffered STM32 signals to its slave inputs. Its VM and VS supply domains are separate, and all grounds are connected. The STM32 side connects only signals and common ground; VS is left unconnected. Do not join the positive 5V computing supply to the positive 6V BEC supply. [Pololu official manual](https://www.pololu.com/docs/0J60/all)
- SN74AHCT125N uses a 5V supply. TI specifies a minimum high-level input voltage of 2.0V, allowing it to accept the STM32's 3.3V PWM. The final wiring must still be checked for power-up defaults, enable states, and backfeeding when either supply is off. [TI datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74ahct125.pdf)
- The mechanical main switch opens the traction-battery circuit independently of high-level software. Removing traction power does not mean the vehicle immediately comes to rest, and radio takeover does not mean stopping-distance acceptance has been completed.

## 5. Selection Rationale and Practical Limits

### Chassis and Traction

The standard TT-02 is a shaft-driven four-wheel-drive platform with servo-steered front wheels. It cannot rotate in place like a differential-drive robot; usable steering angle and minimum turning space must be measured in the room. [Official Tamiya 58631 page](https://www.tamiya.com/english/products/58631/index.html)

When the earlier all-new purchasing proposal was checked, the official 58631 information stated only that a Type540 motor was included, without giving a separate part number or winding turn count. The JJM product page stated that TBLE-02s was supplied free, while the official basic kit description listed the ESC as sold separately, so WP-1060 was specified in that purchasing reference. This paragraph preserves the rationale used at that time. The user has since confirmed that the actual vehicle uses TBLE-04S; current purchasing actions follow the existing-equipment update above.

The current official WP-1060 specifications are 6–9 NiMH cells and a 6V/3A BEC. With 6 NiMH cells, the motor must also have at least 12T or run below 30000rpm at 7.2V. The supplied motor's markings need checking against these requirements. Its reverse modes have different control sequences, so a negative speed command from the higher-level controller cannot simply be treated as immediate reverse motion. [Hobbywing official product page](https://www.hobbywing.com/en/products/quicrun-wp-1060-brushed55), [official manual](https://www.hobbywing.com/en/uploads/file/20240315/4806bbdbab8eb9fea0bb26da87d81b00.pdf)

The Hobbywing global product page uses identifier 30120200, while the French purchasing page checked here uses VAHW30120201. Both are retained unchanged rather than rewritten as the same identifier. The S-U300 supply voltage matches the BEC, but the public documentation does not provide its stall current; therefore, BEC peak-current margin cannot yet be described as verified on the vehicle. [Futaba official page](https://futabausa.com/product/s-u300-standard-servo/)

The Littelfuse holder's 30A rating is an upper limit, not the fuse rating that should be installed. Fuse selection must account for startup current, normal operating current, and the capabilities of the wiring and connectors; it cannot be inferred from the ESC's advertised 60A. [Littelfuse official fuse-holder datasheet](https://www.littelfuse.com/assetdocs/littelfuse-fuse-holder-ato-fhac-datasheet?assetguid=272e0b1a-a576-4173-8740-c1eb469efd79)

### Computing Power Supply

This version selects Pi4B 4GB to use an off-the-shelf 5V/3A power bank and reduce onboard power-integration work. The actual navigation computing load still requires testing. The combined limit for all Pi4 USB peripherals is 1.2A, so lidar startup, NUCLEO, and its sensor loads must be checked together; the complete 5V system must also stay within the supply limits. [Raspberry Pi official power-supply documentation](https://www.raspberrypi.com/documentation/computers/raspberry-pi.html)

The official A1259 dimensions are 104×52.3×26mm, with a weight of 215g, and it supports 5V/3A output. Its low-load shutdown behavior and actual runtime have not been measured; it is not used as a UPS. The kit's 5V/3A desktop supply can be used at different times to power the Pi or charge A1259. [Anker official specifications](https://service.anker.com/product-description/a085g00000GiZgpAAF)

### Sensing and Feedback

BNO085 uses SPI because Adafruit explicitly documents I²C compatibility issues with some controllers and multiplexers. The fact that sensors have I²C interfaces does not mean they can all be connected downstream of TCA9548A. [Adafruit official documentation](https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-9-dof-orientation-imu-fusion-breakout-bno085?view=all)

The four VL53L1X sensors add low-mounted, near-range observations. They do not replace the lidar or guarantee detection of every low, transparent, or lateral obstacle. Detection distance and angle must be evaluated against the actual objects. [Pololu #3415 official specifications](https://www.pololu.com/product/3415)

The optical encoder provides speed and direction feedback; ESC control pulse width cannot stand in for actual speed. After installation, calibrate counts per revolution, transmission ratio, effective tire circumference, and forward/reverse direction. Steering commands must also be calibrated against measured wheel angles. This version does not add a separate steering-angle sensor, and a servo command is not treated as a measured wheel angle. [Seeed official encoder page](https://www.seeedstudio.com/Grove-Optical-Rotary-Encoder-TCUT1600X01.html)

## 6. Included Items and Equipment That Can Be Borrowed

The Pi kit already includes a 32GB microSD, case, desktop power supply, and display cable. The RPLIDAR product includes a USB-UART adapter, and NUCLEO includes its debugger. The chassis kit includes four wheels and the drivetrain. These items do not need duplicate purchases.

The operator uses an existing computer. The first version adds no camera, GPS/UWB, onboard touchscreen, differential-drive motor driver board, or 3D printer; none is required by the currently confirmed requirements.

The laboratory needs an available multimeter, oscilloscope or logic analyzer, current-limited DC supply, soldering tools, and mechanical measurement/fabrication tools. Availability is not yet confirmed, and a complete set of laboratory instruments has not been imposed on the onboard hardware budget. If basic tools need to be purchased, the models checked during this review include [UNI-T UT139C](https://meters.uni-trend.com/product/ut139-series) and [Weller WE1010 230V](https://www.weller-tools.com/sites/default/files/products/documents/WE_TECH-DATA_T0053298392_WE1010BE_EN.pdf).

## 7. Records Still Required Before Purchasing

1. The packing list, motor nameplate, and motor-terminal photographs for the existing or delivered 58631 equipment; do not purchase the chassis again if it has already been bought.
2. The room's floor-plan dimensions, narrowest passage, floor type, and smallest obstacle to detect, to establish the TT-02's turning space and sensor layout.
3. Actual equipment mounting dimensions, total weight, encoder mechanical drawings, and the complete wiring schedule.
4. Traction startup/operating current, maximum steering load, and the complete USB supply current; then select the fuse rating and test shutdown, radio loss, timeout, and restart behavior.

These records turn a list of models into a vehicle that can be wired, assembled, and evaluated against acceptance criteria. Measurements that have not been completed are not recorded as passed.
